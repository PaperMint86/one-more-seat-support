Android Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Android

Last updated: September 26, 2026

One More Seat is published by AbdelHamid Djeraidi under the PaperMint Studio name. This policy describes the Android version distributed through HUAWEI AppGallery. The game does not require a PaperMint Studio account or ask you to enter your name, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Game progress

Puzzle progress, settings, and your hint balance are saved on your device. Android may back up and restore them if system backup is enabled. There is no guaranteed cross-device sync. Uninstalling the game may erase locally saved progress or unused hints.

In-app purchases

Where available, optional purchases are processed through HUAWEI In-App Purchases using your HUAWEI ID. The game checks purchase receipts, records delivered products on your device, and queries HUAWEI for owned purchases to restore Remove Ads and recover pending hint deliveries. HUAWEI handles payment information; PaperMint Studio does not receive your full payment-card details.

Advertising

One More Seat uses Yodo1 MAS and its advertising partners for interstitial ads and optional rewarded hint videos. Depending on your region, device settings, age, and privacy choices, these providers may process device or advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, app activity, crash information, and performance data. They use this information to show and measure ads, prevent fraud, and operate their services. The game presents available privacy and age controls.

Your choices

You can play the core puzzles offline. Ads and purchases require a network connection and may be unavailable. When offered, Remove Ads stops interstitial ads; rewarded hint videos remain optional. You can use the privacy choices presented in the game and applicable Android device settings. You can uninstall the app to remove locally stored game data, subject to any Android backup settings.

Support messages and requests

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any device or purchase information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and investigate the problem. For privacy questions or requests about information you shared with us, contact papermintstudio.support@gmail.com. Requests about information processed by HUAWEI, Yodo1, or their advertising partners may also need to be made through their own privacy controls.

Android support

For help with One More Seat on Android, email papermintstudio.support@gmail.com with your phone model, Android version, and a description of the issue. For a missing purchase, include the HUAWEI order number if you have it. Never send passwords or payment-card details.
