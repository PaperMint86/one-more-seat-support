Google Play Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Google Play

Last updated: September 27, 2026

One More Seat is published by AbdelHamid Djeraidi under the PaperMint Studio name. This policy describes the Android version distributed through Google Play. The game does not require a PaperMint Studio account or ask you to enter your name, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Game progress and local data

Puzzle progress, settings, and your hint balance are stored on your device. Android or Google may back up and restore this data if device backup is enabled. There is no guaranteed cross-device sync. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game may erase locally stored progress and unused hints.

In-app purchases

Where available, optional purchases are processed through Google Play Billing using your Google account. The game checks purchase status, records delivered products on your device, and queries Google Play for owned purchases so eligible purchases can be restored and pending deliveries can be completed. Google handles payment information; PaperMint Studio does not receive your full payment-card details.

Hint packs are consumable purchases. Remove Ads + 50 Hints is a one-time purchase: the Remove Ads entitlement can be restored through Google Play, while included hints are delivered once.

Advertising and third-party services

One More Seat uses Yodo1 MAS and its advertising partners for interstitial ads and optional rewarded hint videos. Depending on your region, device settings, age, and privacy choices, these providers may collect or process device or advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interactions, app activity, diagnostics, crash information, and performance data. They use this information to provide and measure ads, prevent fraud, maintain security, and operate their services. The game presents available privacy and age controls.

How data is shared and protected

Advertising-related data may be shared with Yodo1 MAS and participating advertising partners. Purchase-related data is processed by Google Play. Information sent to these services is transmitted using the security protections provided by Android and the relevant service. PaperMint Studio does not sell information that you send directly to us.

Your choices

You can play the core puzzles offline. Ads and purchases require a network connection and may be unavailable. When purchased, Remove Ads stops interstitial ads; rewarded hint videos remain optional. You can use privacy choices presented in the game and applicable Android or Google settings, including advertising controls.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to any Android or Google backup. PaperMint Studio keeps support emails only as long as reasonably necessary to answer requests, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Google Play, Yodo1, and advertising partners retain and delete data according to their own policies and controls. Because the game does not create a PaperMint Studio account, there is no PaperMint Studio game account to delete.

Support messages and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any device or purchase information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and investigate the problem. For privacy questions or requests concerning information you shared directly with PaperMint Studio, contact papermintstudio.support@gmail.com. Requests concerning information processed by Google, Yodo1, or their advertising partners may also need to be made through their privacy controls.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the game or its services change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

Google Play support

For help with One More Seat on Google Play, email papermintstudio.support@gmail.com with your phone model, Android version, and a description of the issue. For a missing purchase, include the Google Play order number if you have it. Never send passwords or payment-card details.
