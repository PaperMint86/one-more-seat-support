Common Questions

Game progress

Classic gameplay works offline. If optional iCloud syncing is enabled and available, progress may sync between devices through Apple's iCloud services.

Remove Ads purchase

Remove Ads + 50 Hints is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. Its ad-removal entitlement can be restored through Apple on devices signed into the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Hint packs

Purchased hint packs are consumable. After hints have been delivered or used, they cannot normally be restored like a non-consumable purchase.

Purchase problems

If a purchase is not recognized, contact us with your device model, Apple Account region, and approximate purchase date. Do not include passwords or full payment details.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Accounts

One More Seat does not require a PaperMint Studio account or sign-in.

Game data and iCloud

Game progress and settings are primarily stored on your device. If you enable iCloud syncing, relevant progress data may be stored and synchronized using Apple's iCloud services.

Advertising

One More Seat uses the Yodo1 advertising SDK. Depending on your age, consent choices, device settings, and region, Yodo1 and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, approximate location, advertising interactions, usage information, and diagnostics. This information may be used to provide advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud, and operate advertising services.

In-app purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. PaperMint Studio does not receive your complete payment-card information.

Support communications

If you contact us, we receive the email address, message, and any device or diagnostic information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate problems.

Children

The app is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. Advertising choices are handled through the age and privacy-consent experience provided in the app.

Your choices

You can control iCloud and applicable privacy permissions through your device settings. Requests involving information held by Apple, Yodo1, or advertising partners may also be subject to their respective policies and request procedures.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email papermintstudio.support@gmail.com .

You can also review Apple's Privacy Policy .
